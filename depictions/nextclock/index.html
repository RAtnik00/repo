NextClock — 1.1.0

Rootless iOS 15 supported. iOS 16–17 support remains experimental with limited tester reports. Includes arm64 and arm64e binaries. Requires a compatible jailbreak with tweak injection and PreferenceLoader; TrollStore alone is not enough. iOS 13–14 is not supported in this release.

Free custom image-based lock screen clocks for rootless iOS 15.0–15.8.8.

🎃 Happy Halloween!

The new Halloween pack includes transparent pumpkin digits with animated candlelight. Select Halloween in settings.

Make it yours

Static and animated packs, custom pack imports, animation speed, digit size, spacing, vertical offset, time format, optional seconds, date visibility and tint colors.

Rootless iOS 15.0–15.8.8. Includes arm64 and arm64e binaries. Confirmed working on iPhone 7 Plus (iOS 15.8.8), iPhone 12 (iOS 15.3.1), and iPhone 13 Pro Max (iOS 15.1.1). Other devices and iOS 15 versions require testing. The seconds display has been verified on iPhone 7 Plus; it has not yet been separately verified on the other listed devices. English, Ukrainian, Russian, German, Polish, Swedish and Norwegian Bokmål, Italian and French localization.

Changelog

1.1.0 — Halloween & charging fixes

  • Added the animated Halloween pumpkin pack, static fallback and a greeting at the top of settings.
  • Added Italian and French; nine languages in total.
  • Improved charging transitions on iOS 15: hide clocks/date before the battery and restore them after the return animation.
  • Corrected the animation timeline closing frame.
  • Improved .nextclock file selection and validation of malformed ZIP archives.
  • Unified About/diagnostics versions and updated compatibility information.
  • Includes the Circuit pack, modern adapter fixes and wake-up changes from the beta series.

1.1.0 beta 3 — Languages and wake-up testing

  • Added German, Polish, Swedish and Norwegian Bokmål settings translations, selected automatically from the phone language.
  • Experimental wake-up flicker fix: preserve a static digit frame while detached and refresh immediately when the screen/view returns (needs device testing).
  • Includes beta 2 adapter detection, charging/status and wallpaper/music transition changes.
  • Please report repeated wake/sleep behavior, seconds, custom packs and translation/layout issues.

1.1.0 beta 2 — Compatibility fixes

  • Fixed modern adapter selection on systems with setDisplayDate: but no displayDate getter.
  • Capture display dates from the system setter instead of calling a missing getter.
  • Added transient-subtitle detection and guarded subtitle-update hooks to address charging/status overlap (needs device testing).
  • Added per-instance stock-clock visibility handling for wallpaper and music-artwork transitions (needs device testing).
  • Expanded diagnostics with selector availability and instance method names.

1.1.0 beta 1 — Public testing

  • Added an experimental runtime-selected clock adapter for iOS 16–17; not yet verified on devices.
  • Retained the legacy iOS 15 adapter.
  • Added Export diagnostics in Settings for tester reports; no automatic upload or notification text collection.
  • Added the Circuit animated pack with transparent backgrounds and a red colon.
  • Added beta notices in English, Russian and Ukrainian.
  • Built the tweak and preferences bundle for arm64 and arm64e.

Version 1.0.1

  • Added an optional seconds display.
  • Added arm64e binaries for the tweak and preferences bundle.
  • Fixed preferences loading on tested arm64e devices.
  • Localized the seconds switch in English, Russian and Ukrainian.
  • Reset settings now also resets the seconds option.

Version 1.0.0 — First stable release

  • First stable release for rootless iOS 15 (arm64).
  • Custom static PNG and animated GIF/APNG digit packs, including Anime, CRT, Pixel and Sakura.
  • Pack selection, details, import from folders, ZIP and .nextclock archives, and deletion of imported packs.
  • Animation speed control and a still-image option for Low Power Mode.
  • Adjustable digit size, spacing, vertical offset and color tint.
  • System, 12-hour and 24-hour time formats, with optional date hiding.
  • Live preferences with English, Russian and Ukrainian localization.
  • Clock and date visibility follows temporary system status messages, including charging.
  • Settings reset and a SpringBoard restart button.

Promotional screenshots have been translated into English.
