NextClock

Free custom image-based lock screen clocks for rootless iOS 15.

Make it yours

Static and animated packs, custom pack imports, animation speed, digit size, spacing, vertical offset, time format, optional seconds, date visibility and tint colors.

Rootless iOS 15. Includes arm64 and arm64e binaries. Confirmed working on iPhone 7 Plus (iOS 15.8.8), iPhone 12 (iOS 15.3.1), and iPhone 13 Pro Max (iOS 15.1.1). Other devices and iOS 15 versions require testing. The seconds display has been verified on iPhone 7 Plus; it has not yet been separately verified on the other listed devices. English, Ukrainian and Russian localization.

Version 1.0.1

  • Added arm64e builds for the tweak and preferences bundle to fix settings loading on compatible devices.
