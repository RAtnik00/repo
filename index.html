Zone

Unofficial English, Russian and Ukrainian translation for iosdump’s Zone tweak. The original Zone 2.1.0 binaries remain unchanged. Package version: 2.1.0+translation2.

Includes the original author’s icon, translated settings and author credits, plus the original 2.1.0 changes: fixes for the Lock Screen poster panel and black screen, separate video-loop controls for Lock Screen and Home Screen, and other settings/UI fixes.

Upstream lists iOS 14–17 support. This build targets rootless iphoneos-arm64 (/var/jb); compatibility also depends on the jailbreak and tweak-injection environment.
